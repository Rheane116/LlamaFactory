### 1. Target Entity Types
You must identify and extract entities belonging ONLY to the following categories:
* **Task**: Applications, goals, or problems being solved (e.g., machine translation, text classification).
* **Method**: Algorithms, models, systems, or techniques proposed or utilized (e.g., neural networks, SVM, language models).
* **Metric**: Measures or metrics used to evaluate the performance of a method or task (e.g., F1-score, accuracy, BLEU).
* **Material**: Datasets, corpora, or resources used in the research (e.g., English text, images, Penn Treebank).

### 2. Target Relation Types
You must identify relationships between the extracted entities belonging ONLY to the following categories:
* **used-for**: The head entity (e.g., Method/Material) is utilized to accomplish or process the tail entity (e.g., Task/Method).
* **evaluate-for**: The head entity (e.g., Metric) is used to evaluate the tail entity (e.g., Method/Task).
* **feature-of**: The head entity is a characteristic, property, or feature belonging to the tail entity.
* **part-of**: The head entity is a structural component, module, or sub-part of the tail entity.
* **hyponym-of**: The head entity is a specific instance, sub-type, or subclass of the tail entity (i.e., Head "is a kind of" Tail).
* **compare**: The head entity and tail entity are compared against each other. Since this relation is symmetric, the order of the head and tail entities does not affect the semantic meaning.
* **conjunction**: The head entity and tail entity are used in conjunction, coordinated, or treated as parallel/equivalent. This is a symmetric relation.
